# Icons

Twenty-seven drawings, generated by `build.py` from `hand.py` with fixed seeds, so every run gives the same paths.

## Two hands (round 1 of the redesign, 2026-10-30)

- **The field hand** (`hand.py`, second half: `stroke`, `outline`, `ring`, `coil`, `ripple`, `clay_dot`, `drawn`). A field notebook's line: it bows and wanders slowly along its length, a corner is two strokes that cross or fall just short, a ring starts and ends a little apart, a stroke may run on past where it was aimed, and a long stroke is inked twice (a shorter second pass back over its middle, a hair to one side) so it swells there and thins towards its ends. Both passes sit in one path, so the drawing still draws itself with `pathLength="1"`. It draws the nine drawings shown on backtide labs (`progress`, `preview`, `done`, `tape`, `notebook`, `pencil`, `records`, `rewinds`, `holds`), `scale` and the control `play`. `pencil`, `scale` and `play` use it calmer (less wobble, crisp corners): instruments, well made.
- **The steady hand** (`hand.py`, first half, and the helpers in `build.py`): smooth curves with a faint even bow. The other drawings and six controls, until their own round. New drawings use the field hand.
- **The boil.** Every drawing by the field hand is drawn three times: frame 0, the still drawing written in pages and files, and frames 1 and 2, which change only the tremor (`hand.frame(k)`; the shape, where it overshoots and which side it presses, comes from the seed alone). `icons.js` carries them as `b` on each part and `BacktideIcons.frames(name)` returns them in the order the paths are written; `motion/boil.js` steps through them on the public site. The steady hand ignores the frame, so its drawings have none.
- **Two sets (the founder's decision of 2026-10-30).** The public site (backtide labs, `site/`) uses `icons.js`: the field hand and its boil. The product web (`backtide web`) keeps its icons plain and still: it draws from `plain.json`, the steady hand's paths frozen as they were on 2026-10-30, which `build.py` never writes. `tests/test_web_brand.py` pins `backtide/web/templates/icons.html` to `plain.json` (`uv run python -m tests.test_web_brand` rewrites it). A drawing changed in `build.py` therefore reaches the site, never the product web; to change the product web's drawing on purpose, edit `plain.json` (a decision, not a side effect).
- **Reviewing a round:** the icon lab, `site/capturas/iconos/index.html` (served by the `site` entry of `.claude/launch.json` at `/capturas/iconos/`), shows the previous commit's drawings beside the working tree's, at 24, 52, 96 and 160 px, both themes, with the boil, the idle and simulated reduced motion to switch.

## The clay dot

In a drawing by the field hand the clay is at most **one dot**, `clay_dot` (`fill`), of radius `hand.DOT` (2.6 units) in every drawing, never touching the ink (at least about 1.5 units of paper between them at 24 px), and set down last: it is the drawing's full stop, the place the eye should land. The pebble on the cairn, the centre the spiral starts from, the moon's light on the water, the full stop after the logbook's last line, the pencil's point on the paper, the dot that leaves the record's line, the centre the loop turns about, the padlock's keyhole, the balance's pivot. No clay strokes and no pale clay fills in these drawings. One exception has no dot at all: `tape`, backtide's own object, which stands beside the name whose mark already carries the dot. The steady-hand drawings keep their old clay until they are redrawn.

- `svg/<name>.svg`: one file per icon, coloured with the light theme's values (ink, clay mark, line, clay soft), for documents and slides. The stroke there is 1.75px at any size (`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`; these files are never drawn on).
- `icons.js`: the same paths as data, and `BacktideIcons.make(name, extraClasses)`, which builds an `<svg>` node by node with `createElementNS` (never markup). For rows that JavaScript builds.
- In pages built by a script, write the markup in (`icon_svg(name)` in `build.py`); `../build.py` does that for the sample page with `{{ic:name}}`.

Run `python3 brand/design-system/project/icons/build.py` (or the whole `../build.py`).

## Controls

The last seven are the glyphs of a player's bar (the demo film on the site). They follow the same grid, stroke and hand, with two differences: **ink only, no clay**, because they stand together in one row and a player's one clay thing is its play dot or its playhead; and **no gesture**, because they change into one another instead (play and pause, sound and sound off, full screen and back). Each sits inside a real `<button>` whose label says what it does; the glyph is `aria-hidden`. Use them for controls only, never as a section's or a state's picture.

**One exception, `play` (the founder's decision of 2026-10-30):** it is drawn by the field hand and has boil frames. In the film's clay dot on the public site (`player.js`, `make("play", "film__dot-glyph ic--idle")`) it boils while the poster is on screen, like a drawing in the idle layer; it still has no idle movement of its own (no rule in `idle.css`), stays ink only (the film's clay is its dot) and stops with reduced motion or a hidden tab. Only its paths' `d` change, inside an `aria-hidden` svg, so the button's label and focus ring are untouched. The play glyph in the bar and the other six controls never boil.

## In a page

```html
<svg class="ic ic-holds" viewBox="0 0 48 48" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false">
  <g class="ic-g ic-g-shackle"><path class="ic-ink" pathLength="1" d="..."/></g>
  <g class="ic-g ic-g-body"><path class="ic-ink" pathLength="1" d="..."/><path class="ic-fill" d="..."/></g>
</svg>
```

- Classes: `ic-ink` (stroke in ink), `ic-faint` (stroke in `line`), `ic-clay` (stroke in clay mark), `ic-fill` (the clay shape), `ic-soft` (the pale clay fill). The colours follow the theme.
- Sizes: `.ic` 24px, `.ic--m` 36px, `.ic--l` 52px, `.ic--xl` 96px. **The stroke is set in the icon's own units** (`--ic-stroke = 1.75 x 48 / size in px`), so a new size must set its own `--ic-stroke`. `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` is not used inline: with it, Chrome measures the dashes on screen and an icon larger than 48px is drawn only in part.
- `pathLength="1"` on every stroke lets it draw itself (`stroke-dashoffset` 1 to 0) in the entrance and the reveal.
- Put the icon inside an `.ic-host` (a row, a section, a card) for its gesture on hover and keyboard focus.
- On the public site only, put it inside a `data-idle` container (or give it `.ic--idle`) for its slow idle; see `../motion/README.md`.

## The rule for drawing a new icon

So that a new one looks drawn by the same hand (the field hand):

1. **Grid.** 48 x 48 units; keep every stroke inside 4 to 44 (strokes may touch 4.5 to 43.5 for the ground).
2. **Few strokes.** Three to eight. Each one is a `stroke`, an `outline` (a polygon drawn side by side), a `ring`, a `coil` or a `ripple` from `hand.py`, wrapped in `drawn(...)`: lines that bow, overshoot and press, never many noisy points and never a perfect geometric primitive. Keep `press=False` for short and faint strokes.
3. **One weight.** Every stroke is the same 1.75px on screen (the second pass only swells it). No fills of ink. No detail smaller than 2 units. Fill about the same area as the others: a drawing spans roughly 34 to 40 units.
4. **Colour, in this order.** Ink for the drawing; `faint` for the ground, water lines, ruled lines and secondary detail; **one clay dot**, `clay_dot`, by the rule above (none only for `tape`).
5. **The ground.** If the object stands on something, draw a faint line under it (`preview`, `flag`) or water (`done`, `still`).
6. **Seeds.** Give the icon its own block of ten seeds (the next free one is 280; a string such as `"59/2"` when a block runs out). Never reuse a seed: two strokes with the same seed wobble the same way and look stamped. The boil frames come from the same seeds.
7. **A gesture.** Name the groups (`("bell", "ink", ...)` becomes `ic-g-bell`) and give the moving group one gesture in `motion/motion.css`, with its pivot in 48 units: a turn, a hop, a sway, a blink. Once, under 1 s, never looping; add it for `:hover` and for `:focus-visible`. Then its idle in `motion/idle.css` (D34, public site only): a smaller, slower version of the same gesture, `transform` only, with its own period between 7 and 12.5 s that no other drawing uses, moving for at most a quarter of it (`tests/test_site.py` checks all of that).
8. **A meaning.** One line in `MEANING` (`@icon("name", "...")`), and a row in the table above.
9. **Check it** at 18, 24, 36, 52 and 96px on the sample page and in the icon lab, in both themes, before and after its gesture, and boiling.
