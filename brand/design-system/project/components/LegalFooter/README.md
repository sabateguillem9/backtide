# LegalFooter

The footer of every public page: links to the privacy policy and the legal notice, and the language switch, in `micro`.

- Props: `links` (default privacy and legal), `languages` (default `en`, `es`), `current`, `onLanguage(code)`, `languageLabel`, and `ruled` for document pages (hairline above, spread to both sides).
- The active language shows in `ink`, the others in `ink-muted`. Remember the choice in local storage only.
